# Ratings: a personal message (drafts)

Send about a week before launch week, to roughly 15 friends and former
classmates who would actually use the app. A handful of honest ratings makes
a new listing look less empty to everyone who finds it later.

Rules, from Apple and from common sense:

- **No incentives of any kind.** No favours, gifts or swaps in return.
  Apple forbids it.
- Ask for an **honest** rating, never a five-star one.
- Only people who use it. A rating from someone who opened it once doesn't
  help anyone.
- Send it yourself, one person at a time, in your own words. Edit freely.
