# Launch kit (drafts)

Everything here is a draft for Sami to post or send himself. Nothing in this
folder is published, sent or scheduled automatically, and nothing should be.

| File | What it is | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ratings-seed.md](ratings-seed.md) | A personal message asking friends for an honest rating | About a week **before** launch week |
| [show-hn.md](show-hn.md) | Show HN title and first comment | Launch week, one weekday |
| [reddit.md](reddit.md) | r/SideProject and r/iOSApps posts | Launch week, not the same day as Show HN |
| [press-pitch.md](press-pitch.md) | Email to architecture media, English and Czech | Launch week |
| [schools.md](schools.md) | Email to studio tutors and student societies | Launch week, or the start of a semester |
| [instagram.md](instagram.md) | Caption template for posting your own Boards, and a monthly routine | Ongoing |

## Before launch week

- [ ] Send the ratings message to about 15 people who would genuinely use it.
- [ ] Check archipic.app/press.html has the Board examples (see the TODO in it).
- [ ] Set the promotional text for launch (`fastlane/metadata/*/promotional_text.txt`,
      then `tools/fastlane.sh promo_text`). It can change without a release.

## Launch week

- [ ] Show HN on a US weekday morning (around midnight to 2 am in Sydney).
      Stay to answer comments for the first few hours.
- [ ] Reddit posts on different days from Show HN, after reading each
      subreddit's self-promotion rules.
- [ ] Press emails, one outlet at a time, each with its own first line.
- [ ] School emails.
- [ ] Reply to every comment and email for an afternoon.

## Rules for every draft

- Link the press page (`https://archipic.app/press.html`) and the App Store
  (`https://apps.apple.com/app/id6777989742`). Plain URLs only: no tracking or
  campaign parameters, no link shorteners. Never attach files to press emails.
- Never name your employer.
- The bio is "Sami Abdulnour, a Czech architect". The drafts don't say where
  you live or work; add it yourself where it helps (for example in the emails
  to Sydney schools).
- No incentives of any kind for ratings or reviews (Apple forbids them).
- House style: no em-dashes, describe instead of praise, no exclamation marks,
  no emoji, British spelling. See `BUILD-IOS.md` → "Promotion, every release".
