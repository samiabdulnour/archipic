# Press pitch (drafts)

One email per outlet, sent by Sami. Link the press page; **never attach
files** (attachments get filtered and editors prefer links). Find the right
address on each outlet's contact or "submit news" page. Don't guess one.
Personalise the first line for each outlet using the angles below.

Before sending: check the press page has the example Boards (the TODO in
`docs/press.html`).

---

## English template

**Subject:** Archipic, a free private photo journal for architects (iPhone and iPad)

Dear [name],

[One sentence for this outlet, from the angles below.]

Archipic is a free iPhone and iPad app for keeping the architecture you
notice: a façade on a walk, a joint at an exhibition, a plan in a book. Each
photo is tagged in about two taps from a small, fixed vocabulary (Building,
Element or Graphic, then fields such as typology, material or kind), and the
archive can be searched by type, date, project or place. Any selection can be
laid out as a print-ready B1 poster or an A4 journal.

It is private by design. There is no account, no advertising and no
analytics, and photos sync only through the user's own iCloud. The App Store
privacy label reads Data Not Collected. I'm a Czech architect and built it
for my own reference archive, learning to program along the way.

Facts, screenshots, the icon and example Boards are on the press page:
https://archipic.app/press.html

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6777989742

I'm happy to answer questions.

Kind regards,
Sami Abdulnour
sami.abdulnour@gmail.com

## Angles, one per outlet

Use one as the first sentence, in your own words. Each points at something
the outlet's readers would care about; none claims coverage or a
relationship that doesn't exist.

- **ArchDaily.** A tool aimed at architecture students as much as
  practitioners, who make up a large part of the readership. Lead with the
  student use: site visits, study trips, building a precedent library during
  a degree.
- **Dezeen.** The design decision: a fixed vocabulary instead of free tags,
  and a privacy stance (no account, no analytics) in a category of
  cloud-first apps. Lead with the "why", not the feature list.
- **Archinect.** The maker story: a practising architect who learned to
  program to build a tool for the profession. Archinect readers include many
  people thinking about how architects work.
- **designboom.** The visual output: Boards, the justified gallery-wall
  poster and the A4 journal, with captions drawn from the tags. Lead with an
  example Board from the press page.
- **Architizer.** Practice and workflow: how architects keep precedent
  libraries, and the export that carries tags into Lightroom, Bridge and
  InDesign as standard metadata.
