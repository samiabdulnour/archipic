# Instagram: posting your own Boards (drafts)

The idea: post Boards from your own archive as archive content, not ads. Each
post is a set of photos you took, laid out by Archipic. The small
archipic.app mark in the footer does the attribution, so the caption doesn't
need to sell anything.

## Before posting a Board

- **Check the captions.** Board captions come from your tags, including the
  place and the **project name**. Don't publish client project names or
  anything under an NDA: rename the project or leave those photos out.
- Use **Post · 4:5** for the feed (it takes the most screen space) and
  **Story · 9:16** for stories. Keep the mark switched on.

---

## Caption template

```
[title, lower case like the Board captions]

[One or two sentences: what links these photos and where you took them.]

[N] photos, [place], [month year]. Laid out in Archipic.
```

Optional last line, on its own: three to five plain descriptive hashtags,
never a sentence made of hashtags. For example `#architecture #brickwork
#prague`.

**Example (replace everything in brackets with your own):**

```
[stairs of the old town]

[Seven stairs photographed over one weekend, from a cellar step to a
cathedral spiral. Most are stone; two are timber added much later.]

[7] photos, [place], [month year]. Laid out in Archipic.
```

Same house style as everything else: no em-dashes, no emoji, no exclamation
marks, describe instead of praise.

---

## Monthly routine (one sitting, about 90 minutes)

1. **Pick.** In Archipic, filter by the past month, or open one project or
   trip. Choose 8 to 12 sets of 5 to 10 photos each.
2. **Make Boards.** For each set: Select → Board → **Post · 4:5**, give it a
   title, export the image. Make a **Story · 9:16** version of the best two
   or three.
3. **Write captions** with the template above, in one go, in a notes file.
4. **Schedule** in Meta Business Suite → Planner: two or three posts a week,
   spread across the month, at times your followers are online (Insights
   shows when).
5. **Check once a week** for comments and reply. That's the only part that
   can't be scheduled.
